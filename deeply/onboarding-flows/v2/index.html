New-user onboarding — A (signs up) and B (invited) · v2

v2 is a density pass on the approved v1. The flow, steps, order and saved data are the same. Each screen just says less.

New not in the app today Changed exists, reshaped or moved Existing ships today, unchanged

A makes a Commitment, schedules the first weekly check-in, gives a phone number, then invites B. B arrives after the schedule exists, so B confirms instead of creating, makes their own Commitment, and sees a reveal.

Density rules

  1. No eyebrow that repeats the title. The shell header and progress dots stay.
  2. One title plus at most one supporting line (about 12 words).
  3. A preview only where the preview is the payoff: the Commitment card and the reveal.
  4. At most 6 visible choices per decision. The rest sit behind “More ideas”.
  5. Defaults over pickers. A good default is one row with “Change”.

What changed from v1

  • Us (A): one question open at a time. Answers collapse to a chip line with Edit. No preview.
  • More of (A, B): the question is now the title. 6 choices + Something else. No preview.
  • One thing (A, B): 4 idea rows, Write your own, More ideas. The picked row becomes the Commitment card.
  • In return (A): shorter title. 4 rows, with “Sam decides” on the picked row. No preview.
  • Schedule (A): one “Sunday · 8:00 PM” row with Change replaces the day picker, time select and preview.
  • Reminder (A, B): one-line sub. Consent text moves under the button. No SMS preview.
  • Invite (A): no locked slots or suggestion line. The note is a link.
  • Invite landing (B): paragraph cut to two sentences.
  • Reveal (B): sub cut to one line.
  • Schedule (B): one sub line. No preview.
  • Code (A, B): eyebrow removed, nothing else. Name, Partner and Home are unchanged.

Idea library (draft — edit freely)

The One thing and In return cards show the ideas for the chosen “More of”. “Something else” mixes all categories. Alex’s own pick is never offered back in “In return”. Ideas marked * appear only when intake facts match.

Time together 8 ideas

  • Plan one evening just for us
  • Phones away at dinner
  • A walk together, just us
  • Plan our next date
  • Cook a meal together
  • Morning coffee, no screens
  • Keep one night free of other plans
  • Try something new together

Feeling close 8 ideas

  • Ask about their day, and really listen
  • A long hug when we see each other
  • Send a note saying why I love them
  • Go to bed at the same time
  • Share something I’ve been thinking about
  • Plan a small surprise
  • Hold hands on a walk
  • Say “I love you” and mean it

Sharing the load 8 ideas

  • Take one chore off their plate
  • Handle dinner one night, start to finish
  • Own one recurring task this week
  • Plan the week together on Sunday
  • Notice what needs doing without being asked
  • Give them an hour to themselves
  • Handle an errand they’ve been putting off
  • Take the kids one morning*

How we talk 8 ideas

  • 10 minutes a day, no phones
  • Listen without fixing
  • Say what I need instead of hinting
  • Bring up one thing gently, at a calm moment
  • Check in before making plans
  • Pause when things get heated
  • Ask one real question each day
  • Tell them one thing I appreciate each day

Fun & play 8 ideas

  • Game night
  • Try a new restaurant or recipe
  • Revisit a place from early on
  • Plan a mini adventure
  • Dance in the kitchen
  • Watch something we both pick
  • Learn something new together
  • Laugh about something silly together

Feeling appreciated 6 ideas

  • Thank them for something specific
  • Notice and name one thing they did
  • Leave a note where they’ll find it
  • Brag about them to someone, with them there
  • Celebrate a small win
  • Tell them what I admire about them

*Only when the Us card says they have children.
